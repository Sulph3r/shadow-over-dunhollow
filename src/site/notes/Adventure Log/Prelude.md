The adventure begins on a road in the land of [[Locations/Ustalav\|Ustalav]].

The members of the party are hired as mercenaries by the [[Orginizations/Knights of Lastwall\|Knights of Lastwall]] tasked with escorting the physician [[NPCs/Ugin Petri\|Ugin Petri]] form the city of [[Locations/Vauntil\|Vauntil]] to [[Locations/Dunhollow\|Dunhollow]] .

The town is in need of more healers, there are rumors of a [[Quests/Strange Illness\|Strange Illness]] plaguing the residents of [[Locations/Dunhollow\|Dunhollow]].
