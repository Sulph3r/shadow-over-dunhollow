The adventure begins on a road in the land of [[Locations/Ustalav\|Ustalav]].

The members of the party are hired as mercenaries by the [[Knights of Westwall\|Knights of Westwall]] tasked with escorting the physician [[Ugin Petri\|Ugin Petri]] form the city of [[Vauntil\|Vauntil]] to [[Dunhollow\|Dunhollow]] .

The town is in need of more healers, there are rumors of a [[Strange Illness\|Strange Illness]] plaguing the residents of [[Dunhollow\|Dunhollow]].
