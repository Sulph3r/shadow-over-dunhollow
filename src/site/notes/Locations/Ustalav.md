- The Immortal Principality of Ustalav is a fog-shrouded land situated in the [[Eye of Dread\|Eye of Dread]]. It is divided into 13 counties.

- The nation is home to many horrors such as [[Vampire Lords\|Vampire Lords]] and the undead.

- The once-proud realm suffered under the clawed hands of the [[Whispering Tyrant\|Whispering Tyrant]] for centuries
